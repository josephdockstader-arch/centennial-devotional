The Resurrection and the Life

Lazarus, Bethany, and the Road to Jerusalem

Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live.

John 11:25

Date to be Posted · Required reading · Questions

The title

Martha meets him on the road outside Bethany and says the thing she has clearly been saying for four days: if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died. He tells her that her brother shall rise again, and she gives the correct Sunday answer, that she knows he shall rise in the resurrection at the last day. She is not wrong. She is just holding it at a safe distance, out in the future, where it cannot help her this week. And he answers her by moving it: I am the resurrection, and the life. Not it will happen, but I am standing here. Then he asks her, believest thou this? That is the question this cohort is built on. Notice also where this sits in the story. The raising of Lazarus is the last public miracle in John, and John tells us it is what filled the road with palm branches a few days later and what hardened the decision to kill him. The same act brought the crowds out and set the execution in motion.

Required reading, for every speaker in this cohort

  • John 11, straight through
  • John 12:1-19
  • Alma 40:11-14 and 21-23
  • Matthew 21:1-17
  • Zechariah 9:9

Questions for thought

  1. Verse 35 is the shortest verse in the Bible. He knew Lazarus would be walking out within the hour. Why weep?
  2. Martha's answer about the last day is completely correct doctrine. Why does he correct it anyway?
  3. He stayed two days after hearing Lazarus was sick. Find every explanation the text itself offers before you reach for one it does not.
  4. Find out what a first-century burial involved and why the fourth day was significant to the people standing there.
  5. He tells the bystanders to loose him and let him go. Why does the man still need help after the miracle?
  6. John 11:53 says from that day they took counsel to put him to death. Raising a man from the dead is what got him killed. Why would that be the last straw?
  7. He sends for a specific animal so that Zechariah 9:9 would be fulfilled. He arranged it. What was he claiming, and who in that road would have caught it?
  8. The same crowd that shouted Hosanna was in the city that Friday. What do you make of that?
  9. Where in your life are you holding a true doctrine out at a safe distance in the future?
