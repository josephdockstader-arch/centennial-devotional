Cohort Sixteen · Week Of March 1 · Eight Talks

The Son of God

Who Say Ye That I Am

And Simon Peter answered and said, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God.

Matthew 16:16

The talks

  1. Office What the title claimed in that world, and why it was dangerous. Matthew 16:16, and then John 5:18, where they sought to kill him because he called God his Father, making himself equal with God. Tell us what his enemies understood him to be saying.
  2. Case All three of the first Gospels put the same three things in the same order: the confession, the first plain announcement that he would be killed, and then the transfiguration. Find out why that sequence and show us the argument it makes.
  3. Epistle 1 Corinthians 12:3, "no man can say that Jesus is the Lord, but by the Holy Ghost." Peter is told his answer came by revelation, not by flesh and blood. Paul says the same thing about anyone who says it. Build your talk on that.
  4. Restoration Keys. Matthew 16:19, D&C 128:20, and D&C 110, where Moses and Elias and Elijah, the same beings who appeared on the mount, come to the Kirtland Temple and deliver keys. Show us what the transfiguration was for.
  5. Text Matthew 16:13-28. Work the whole passage, including the part we skip: six verses after Peter is called blessed, he is called Satan. Do not stop at the confession.
  6. World Caesarea Philippi. Find out what was at that place, the rock face, the grotto sacred to Pan, the city Herod Philip built there. Then tell us why he chose that spot to ask who men said he was.
  7. Witness Peter, who gets it exactly right and is called blessed, and then within minutes is rebuked harder than anyone else in the Gospels. Take him through the questions on the facing page.
  8. Objection He was charged with blasphemy and the charge stuck. John 8:48-59 and the temple confrontations. Was he claiming to be God, and what exactly did his enemies think they heard? Answer plainly.

The title

He does not open with the question about himself. He asks first what other people are saying, and the disciples give him the rumors: John the Baptist, Elias, Jeremias, one of the prophets. Every one of those answers is respectful and every one of them is wrong. Then he narrows it: but whom say ye that I am? Peter answers, thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God, and he is told that flesh and blood did not reveal it to him. That is the hinge of the whole cohort. The answer was not available by observation. People had watched him for three years, seen the miracles, heard the sermons, and still landed on prophet. Notice too what the title did to his enemies. In John 5, calling God his Father was heard as making himself equal with God, and they tried to kill him for it. Nobody in that culture heard Son of God as a compliment or a poetic flourish. They heard a claim, and they were right to. This week the school hears that claim asked, answered, confirmed from heaven, and prosecuted.

Required reading, for every speaker in this cohort

  • Matthew 16:13-28 and 17:1-13
  • John 5:16-30
  • John 8:48-59
  • D&C 110
  • 2 Peter 1:16-21

Questions for thought

  1. Every wrong answer the crowd gives is a compliment. Why is a high opinion of him still the wrong answer?
  2. He tells Peter that flesh and blood did not reveal it. What is the difference between concluding something and having it revealed?
  3. Peter is called blessed and then called Satan within the same conversation. What changed?
  4. Find out what was at Caesarea Philippi. Why hold that conversation in front of a pagan shrine?
  5. Immediately after the confession he tells them he must be killed. Why is that the first thing he says once they know who he is?
  6. Peter, James, and John were told to say nothing about the transfiguration until after the resurrection. Why the silence?
  7. In 2 Peter 1:16-21 Peter refers back to that mountain, and then says there is something more sure than what he saw. What is it, and why would he rank it higher than an eyewitness experience?
  8. In John 8:58 he says "before Abraham was, I am," and they pick up stones. Find out why that sentence and not the others.
  9. If someone asked you today, without warning, whom say ye that I am, what would come out of your mouth?
