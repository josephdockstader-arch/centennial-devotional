The Light of the World

He Is Risen

Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.

John 8:12

About this name

Jesus says it in the temple treasury, during the feast of tabernacles (John 8:12, 20). The Mishnah describes great lamps lit in that court for the feast, bright enough to light the streets of the city. The Mishnah was compiled around AD 200, so it tells us about the festival rather than about that particular day, but the saying was made where lamps were burning. He says it again in John 9:5, just before giving sight to a man born blind, which puts the claim where it can be watched.

He pairs the light with life. Followers are promised the light of life (John 8:12). In 3 Nephi a voice out of the darkness says “I am the light and the life of the world” (3 Nephi 9:18), spoken while the destruction is still fresh and before the Resurrection; he appears at Bountiful afterward and names himself in person (3 Nephi 11:10–11). D&C 88:6–13 describes that light as filling and governing all things, and as the light by which a person understands anything at all.

  • 3 Nephi 15:9; D&C 88:6–13, 49–50; Moroni 7:15–19.
  • Mishnah Sukkah 5:2–4, on the lamps lit in the temple court for the feast of tabernacles. Compiled around AD 200, so it describes the festival and not that particular day.
  • Luke 24; Matthew 28:1–10; Mark 16:1–8.
  • Leviticus 23:9–14; 1 Corinthians 15.

Questions for research and discussion

  1. Research the feast of tabernacles and the lamps lit in the temple court, using Mishnah Sukkah 5:2–4. What happened at that ceremony? John 8:20 says Jesus was in the treasury when he said it. What does the setting add to the saying?
  2. In John 9 a man born blind is given sight, and the chapter ends with the Pharisees being told they are blind. Who can see in that chapter and who cannot? What decides it?
  3. Read Isaiah 9:2, 42:6–7, 49:6 and 60:1–3. What is light doing in each passage, and who is it for? Follow one of those images into John and see what changes.
  4. The voice in 3 Nephi 9:18 says “I am the light and the life of the world” to people who have just sat through three days of darkness. What does the claim mean said at that moment?
  5. D&C 88:6–13 describes a light that fills space, gives life to all things, and is the light by which a person understands anything. Compare it with John 1:4–9. Are these the same thing?
  6. In Matthew 5:14–16 he calls the disciples the light of the world. What does he hand over to them, and what stays his?
  7. Moroni 7:15–19 says the light of Christ is given to every person so they can tell good from evil. Research what that means for someone who has never heard of Christ. What does the passage settle and what does it leave open?
  8. John 12:42–43 describes people who believed him and said nothing, because of what others would think. Read it beside 12:35–36. What is the warning about walking while you have the light?

Questions for reflection

These are for the speaker, not for the meeting. Write your answers before the discussion or while you are drafting. Nobody will collect them.

  1. When have you kept quiet about something you believed, for the reason given in John 12:43?
  2. If the light of Christ is in every person, what does that change about the one person you find hardest to be around?
