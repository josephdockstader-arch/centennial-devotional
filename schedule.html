The Schedule

How the year runs

The year moves through twenty-two names of Christ, beginning the second week of October. Nineteen names are carried by student cohorts and three by featured speakers. The full order is at the bottom of this page.

Dates are posted about four weeks ahead, once they are certain. If your cohort has started meeting, you can count on speaking in the fourth week.

The four-week cycle

Every cohort runs the same cycle.

  1. Week one The cohort meets for the first time. The reading is assigned, the questions are opened, and each speaker leaves with a direction for research of their own.
  2. Week two The research comes back to the group, the drafts get written, and the cohort holds its read-through.
  3. Week three Practice. Every speaker rehearses, including time on the microphone and the teleprompter.
  4. Week four The cohort gives the devotionals.
